How much insulation is too much?

Critical radius of insulation — cylindrical system · Iman Farahbakhsh · Microteaching, Thermofluids

Conduction resistance, R'cond Convection resistance, R'conv Total resistance, R'tot = R'cond + R'conv

Key idea

Tube radius ri = 5 mm, insulation conductivity k = 0.055 W/(m·K), outer convection coefficient h = 5 W/(m²·K) (cellular-glass insulation, Incropera Example 3.6). The critical radius is rcr = k/h = 11 mm, i.e. an insulation thickness of 6 mm.

Below rcr, adding insulation increases heat transfer (R'cond grows slower than R'conv shrinks). Above rcr, adding insulation decreases heat transfer. Drag the slider — the marker on R'tot (dark curve) traces the minimum at t = 6 mm.
